# Comprehensive Meme Coin Trading Knowledge Base

## 🎯 Core Trading Principles

### Risk Management (CRITICAL)
- **Never invest more than you can afford to lose**
- Allocate only 5-10% of total portfolio to meme coins
- Diversify across multiple tokens (don't go all-in on one)
- Use position sizing: smaller positions for higher risk tokens
- **Set stop losses**: Cut losses at -20% from entry
- **Use trailing stops**: 15% trailing stop to lock in profits

### Capital Protection Rules
- Take profit at regular intervals (don't wait for the moon)
- Recover initial investment after 100% gain (or 50% for large positions)
- Never FOMO into a pump that's already 5x or higher
- Maximum 3-5% of capital per trade for high-risk meme coins

## 📊 Technical Analysis for Meme Coins

### Essential Indicators
1. **Volume Analysis** (MOST IMPORTANT)
   - Current volume > 2x the 10-period average = strong interest
   - High volume confirms price movements
   - Volume spike without price increase = potential dump coming
   - Low volume = consolidation or loss of interest

2. **Moving Averages**
   - 10, 20, 30 EMA for short-term trends
   - 50, 100, 200 SMA for major support/resistance
   - Price above all MAs = strong uptrend
   - Death cross (50 MA below 200 MA) = avoid

3. **Momentum Oscillators**
   - RSI (14): Below 30 = oversold (potential buy), Above 70 = overbought (take profit)
   - MACD (12, 26, 9): Bullish crossover = entry signal
   - ADX > 25 = strong trend confirmation
   - Stochastic: Extreme readings indicate reversals

4. **Volume Patterns**
   - Sudden 3x-5x volume spike = whale activity or news
   - Declining volume during uptrend = weakening momentum
   - Rising volume during downtrend = capitulation (possible bottom)

## 🚀 Early Token Identification (Pump.fun)

### What to Look For (GREEN FLAGS)
1. **Strong Social Media Traction**
   - Organic meme creation (not forced)
   - Growing Twitter/X mentions and engagement
   - Community-created content (art, videos, jokes)
   - Active Telegram/Discord with real conversations (not bots)
   - Influencer attention (but verify it's not paid)

2. **On-Chain Validation**
   - Trading volume confirms social hype
   - Multiple buyers (not just 2-3 wallets)
   - Consistent buy pressure over 15-30 minutes
   - Fast-growing liquidity pools
   - 5+ transactions per minute during launch

3. **Quality Signals**
   - Unique character/identity (not a copycat)
   - Strong memeability (can it go viral?)
   - Clear theme or narrative
   - Professional-looking artwork/branding
   - Whitepaper or roadmap (bonus, but not required)

4. **Bonding Curve Progress**
   - Tokens approaching $69K market cap milestone
   - Steady climb (not instant jump = wash trading)
   - Raydium migration imminent = momentum signal

### Critical RED FLAGS (AVOID IMMEDIATELY)
1. **Wallet Concentration**
   - Top 10 holders own >50% of supply = RUG RISK
   - Single wallet controls >20% = DUMP INCOMING
   - Dev wallet not burned/locked = SCAM

2. **Liquidity Issues**
   - Unlocked liquidity = instant rug possible
   - Liquidity held by single wallet = RED FLAG
   - No liquidity lock after Raydium migration = AVOID
   - Thin liquidity (<$10K) = manipulation risk

3. **Smart Contract Red Flags**
   - Ownership not renounced = dev can change rules
   - Minting function enabled = unlimited dilution possible
   - Hidden tax/fee functions = honeypot
   - Not audited = unknown vulnerabilities
   - Honeypot detection tools flag it = SCAM

4. **Social/Team Red Flags**
   - Anonymous team with no history = higher risk
   - Aggressive marketing with unrealistic promises
   - "100x guaranteed" language = scam
   - Paid shills flooding comments = pump and dump
   - Bot-generated social engagement
   - No previous successful projects
   - Copy-paste of other successful tokens

5. **Trading Pattern Red Flags**
   - Only buy orders, no sells = honeypot
   - Instant 10x-50x pump at launch = wash trading
   - Bundled buys (multiple buys same second) = coordinated dump
   - Unusual patterns: all buys same size = bot manipulation

## 🎯 Entry Strategies

### Strategy 1: Limit Orders (Patience Strategy)
- Set limit orders below current price
- Wait for dips instead of chasing pumps
- Better to miss a moon than buy the top
- Scale in: 25% → 25% → 50% on dips

### Strategy 2: Early Launch Sniper
- Monitor new Pump.fun launches
- Check contract immediately with RugCheck
- Verify dev history and social proof
- Enter small position (<2% portfolio) if green flags
- Must be within first 5-10 minutes
- Exit if no momentum within 30 minutes

### Strategy 3: Momentum Breakout
- Wait for token to establish base (2-4 hours)
- Enter on volume breakout above resistance
- Confirm with RSI < 70 (not overbought yet)
- Higher win rate but miss biggest early gains

### Strategy 4: Accumulation on Dip
- Token already proven (survived 24+ hours)
- Wait for 30-50% correction from ATH
- Buy when volume decreases (accumulation)
- Lower risk, moderate reward

## 💰 Exit Strategies (MOST IMPORTANT)

### The 3-5-Hold Strategy
- Sell 50% at 3x profit
- Sell 25% at 5x profit
- Hold 25% for potential moonshot (10x-100x)
- Guaranteed profit, unlimited upside potential

### The Safe Exit
- Sell 100% initial investment at 2x
- Let profits ride risk-free
- Set trailing stop at -15% from peak

### The Scalper Exit
- 2x = sell 30%
- 3x = sell 30%
- 5x = sell 30%
- 10x = sell remaining 10%

### Emergency Exit Signals
